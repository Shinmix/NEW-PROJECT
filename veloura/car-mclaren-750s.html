McLaren 750S

The lightest and most powerful McLaren Super Series car, and the last to be powered purely by petrol.

Overview

The 750S is the successor to the McLaren 720S and, according to McLaren, the final model powered solely by an internal combustion engine. Its 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 produces 750 PS at 7,500 rpm and 800 Nm at 5,500 rpm.

Design

Although it is a development of the 720S, around a third of the components are new. The dihedral doors, teardrop cabin and hollow headlight channels that feed air to the radiators are all McLaren signatures, and the body sits on a carbon fibre Monocage tub.

Performance

McLaren quotes 0-100 km/h in 2.8 seconds, 0-200 km/h in 7.2 seconds and a top speed of 332 km/h. Shorter gearing, lighter wheels and revised suspension make the 750S feel sharper than the car it replaced.

Technology

Proactive Chassis Control III links the dampers hydraulically instead of using conventional anti-roll bars, letting the car stay flat in corners while remaining comfortable on the road. An active rear wing doubles as an airbrake under heavy braking.

History

Announced in April 2023, the 750S continues the Super Series line that began with the 650S and 720S. It is offered as a coupe and as a Spider with a retractable hard top.

Interesting Facts

  • Every McLaren road car is built around a carbon fibre tub, an idea taken from the team's Formula One cars.
  • The Spider version weighs only about 49 kg more than the coupe.
  • McLaren quotes a lightest dry weight of 1,277 kg for the coupe.
