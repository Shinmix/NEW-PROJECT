History

Bruce McLaren founded his racing team in 1963. The company built the legendary McLaren F1 road car in the 1990s, and McLaren Automotive was established in 2010 as a dedicated road-car manufacturer.

Every McLaren road car is built around a carbon fibre tub, an approach taken directly from the team's Formula One experience.

Known for

  • Carbon fibre monocoque chassis
  • The three-seat McLaren F1
  • Lightweight engineering
