Rolls-Royce Spectre

Rolls-Royce's first electric car, a silent two-door grand tourer with coach doors.

Overview

Launched in 2023, the Spectre is the first fully electric Rolls-Royce and rides on the same aluminium architecture as the Phantom and Cullinan. Two motors produce a combined 430 kW (585 PS) and 900 Nm, with a WLTP range of around 530 km.

Design

The Spectre is a two-door coupe with rear-hinged coach doors and the widest grille ever fitted to a Rolls-Royce, illuminated from within. The Spirit of Ecstasy was reshaped into a lower, more aerodynamic form for this car.

Performance

Electric motors suit the brand unusually well: peak torque is available immediately and delivery is completely smooth. Rolls-Royce quotes 0-100 km/h in 4.5 seconds, with top speed limited to 250 km/h.

Technology

The battery pack is used as an extra layer of sound and vibration insulation between the passengers and the road. Decentralised control software lets the car adjust steering, suspension and braking together based on navigation data about the road ahead.

History

Charles Rolls predicted as early as 1900 that electric cars would be 'perfectly noiseless and clean', and Rolls-Royce has used that quote to frame the Spectre as the fulfilment of a century-old idea. The company intends to sell only electric cars from 2030.

Interesting Facts

  • The Spectre has an EPA-rated range of about 264 miles (425 km).
  • A Black Badge version raises output to 485 kW (659 PS).
  • Its doors can be closed electrically from inside the car at the touch of a button.
