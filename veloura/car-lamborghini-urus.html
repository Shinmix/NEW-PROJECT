Lamborghini Urus S

Overview

Introduced in December 2017 and updated as the Urus S in 2022, the Urus is Lamborghini's high-performance luxury SUV. It uses a 4.0-litre twin-turbo V8 rather than the naturally aspirated V10 and V12 engines found in the brand's sports cars.

Design

The Urus wears Lamborghini's angular design language on a raised body: sharp creases, a hexagonal theme repeated throughout the cabin, and a roofline that slopes far more than most SUVs. It is built on the Volkswagen Group MLB Evo platform shared with the Audi Q7, Bentley Bentayga and Porsche Cayenne.

Performance

Lamborghini quotes 0-100 km/h in 3.5 seconds and a top speed of 305 km/h. Torque vectoring on the rear axle and four-wheel steering help disguise the car's size and weight.

Technology

Driving modes cover road, sport and track settings alongside off-road programmes for snow, sand and gravel, with air suspension adjusting ride height for each. At launch it used the largest carbon-ceramic brake discs fitted to any production car, measuring 440 mm at the front.

History

The Urus concept was shown at the 2012 Beijing Auto Show and reached production in 2017, making it Lamborghini's first SUV since the LM002 of the 1980s. It quickly became the company's best-selling model, passing 10,000 units by mid-2020.

Interesting Facts

  • Its name comes from the aurochs, the wild ancestor of modern domestic cattle.
  • The LM002 of 1986 was Lamborghini's first attempt at an off-roader and used a Countach V12.
  • A plug-in hybrid version, the Urus SE, joined the range in 2024.
