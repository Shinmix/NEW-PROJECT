Aston Martin

Elegant grand tourers with a cinematic reputation.

Founded
1913
Founder
Lionel Martin and Robert Bamford
Headquarters
Gaydon, United Kingdom

History

Aston Martin was founded in 1913 and took its name from the Aston Hill hill climb where Lionel Martin raced. The DB series, named after post-war owner David Brown, produced some of the most admired grand tourers ever built.

The brand's profile grew enormously through its long association with the James Bond films, beginning with the DB5 in Goldfinger.

Known for

  • The DB5 and the James Bond connection
  • Front-engine grand tourers
  • Hand-built V12 engines
