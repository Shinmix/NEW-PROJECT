Performance

Powerful engines, rapid acceleration, precise handling and the engineering that makes it repeatable lap after lap.

Advanced driving, safety, entertainment and performance systems working quietly in the background.

Premium materials, precision assembly and the kind of attention to detail that only shows up close.

Did You Know?

Automotive Facts

Small pieces of history and engineering trivia from the world of luxury cars.

Rolls-Royce

The Ghost nameplate honours the 1906 Silver Ghost, a car whose reliability record helped build the company's early reputation.

Lamborghini

Ferruccio Lamborghini built tractors before he built sports cars, and the company still exists today as a separate tractor manufacturer.

Ferrari

Ferrari's first car under its own name was the 125 S of 1947, powered by a 1.5-litre V12.
