Lamborghini Revuelto

Lamborghini's first V12 plug-in hybrid flagship and the successor to the Aventador.

Overview

Unveiled on 29 March 2023, the Revuelto replaced the Aventador as Lamborghini's V12 flagship. A new 6,498 cc naturally aspirated V12 producing 825 PS works with three electric motors for a combined 1,015 PS, making it the most powerful series-production Lamborghini road car to date.

Design

The Revuelto keeps the scissor doors and wedge profile that define Lamborghini flagships, adds Y-shaped daytime running lights, and leaves the engine bay almost completely exposed under glass. Its monocoque is made entirely from carbon fibre and is around ten per cent lighter than the Aventador's structure.

Performance

Lamborghini quotes 0-100 km/h in 2.5 seconds and a top speed above 350 km/h. Two of the three electric motors drive the front axle, giving torque vectoring and full electric all-wheel drive without any mechanical link to the front wheels.

Technology

A 3.8 kWh battery pack sits in the central tunnel and supports a short electric-only range. Thirteen driving modes combine three drive settings with four hybrid strategies, including a city mode named Citta.

History

The Revuelto is named after a Spanish fighting bull that fought in Barcelona in the 1880s, continuing Lamborghini's bull-naming tradition. Full production began in July 2023, with the first deliveries late that year.

Interesting Facts

  • The V12 revs to 9,250 rpm, where it makes its peak power.
  • Lamborghini rotated the gearbox to sit behind the engine, freeing space for the battery in the tunnel.
  • Orders filled the first two years of production before deliveries even began.
