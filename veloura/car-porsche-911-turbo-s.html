Porsche 911 Turbo S

The everyday supercar: rear-engined, all-wheel drive and usable in any weather.

Overview

Introduced in March 2020, the 992-generation 911 Turbo S uses a 3,745 cc twin-turbocharged flat-six rated at 478 kW (650 PS) and 800 Nm. Unlike most supercars it keeps its engine behind the rear axle, a layout the 911 has used since 1963.

Design

The Turbo S is 45 mm wider than the standard 911 across the rear, with the distinctive air intakes ahead of the rear wheels. Active aerodynamics include an adaptive front spoiler and a large extending rear wing that also works as an airbrake.

Performance

Porsche quotes 0-100 km/h in 2.7 seconds and a top speed of 330 km/h. Traction from the all-wheel-drive system and the weight over the rear axle is what makes the launch so repeatable, even in poor conditions.

Technology

Standard equipment includes ceramic composite brakes, rear-axle steering and Porsche Dynamic Chassis Control active anti-roll bars. The eight-speed PDK gearbox shifts in a fraction of a second without interrupting drive.

History

The Turbo badge dates back to the 930 of 1975, the first turbocharged production 911 and a car famous for its wide body and demanding handling. Successive generations added all-wheel drive and electronic aids that made the Turbo far easier to drive quickly.

Interesting Facts

  • The 911 has been in continuous production since 1963, longer than almost any other sports car.
  • The original 930 Turbo earned the nickname 'the widowmaker' because of its sudden turbo boost.
  • The 992 Turbo S carries rear tyres that are 315 mm wide.
