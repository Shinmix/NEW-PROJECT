History

Porsche started as an engineering consultancy in 1931 and only began building cars under its own name with the 356 in 1948. The 911 arrived in 1963 and has remained in production ever since, evolving generation by generation while keeping its rear-engine layout.

Porsche also applies the same engineering approach to sedans, SUVs and, more recently, to fully electric cars such as the Taycan.

Known for

  • The rear-engine 911
  • Le Mans endurance racing success
  • The Taycan electric sports sedan
