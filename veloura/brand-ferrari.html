History

Ferrari began as Scuderia Ferrari, the racing team Enzo Ferrari founded in 1929 to run cars for Alfa Romeo. The company only became a car manufacturer in its own right after the Second World War, and the 125 S of 1947 is recognised as the first car to carry the Ferrari name.

Every road car since has been shaped by the same idea: the racing programme develops the technology, and the road cars inherit it. Hybrid systems, paddle-shift gearboxes and aerodynamic devices all reached Ferrari showrooms after being proven in competition.

Known for

  • Formula One's longest-running constructor
  • V12 and V6 hybrid road cars
  • The prancing horse emblem
