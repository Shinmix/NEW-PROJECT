About Veloura

Explore the World of Automotive Excellence.

What Veloura is

Veloura is an informational website about luxury automobiles. It exists to explain what makes these cars interesting: their history, their specifications, the engineering behind their performance, the technology they introduced and the craftsmanship that goes into building them.

What Veloura is not

Veloura is not a dealership and not an online shop. There are no prices, no purchase buttons, no financing and no sales enquiries anywhere on the site. Nothing here is sold.

About the information

Specifications are taken from manufacturer figures and published reference sources, and figures can vary between markets and model years. Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons and Unsplash. Veloura is not affiliated with any of the manufacturers featured.
