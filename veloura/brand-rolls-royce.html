History

Charles Rolls and Henry Royce joined forces in 1904, and the Silver Ghost of 1906 earned the company its reputation for reliability and refinement. Rolls-Royce Motor Cars has been owned by BMW since 2003 and builds its cars at Goodwood in England.

Modern Rolls-Royce models are defined by hand-finished interiors, the Spirit of Ecstasy mascot and an obsession with removing noise and vibration from the driving experience.

Known for

  • Spirit of Ecstasy mascot
  • Starlight headliner interiors
  • Bespoke, made-to-order cars
