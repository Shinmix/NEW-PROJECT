Luxury Sedans

Refined saloons and grand tourers designed around comfort, silence and long-distance travel.

Luxury sedans and grand tourers are built for effortless travel. Engineers focus on ride comfort, noise isolation, hand-finished interiors and smooth, high-torque engines rather than outright lap times.
