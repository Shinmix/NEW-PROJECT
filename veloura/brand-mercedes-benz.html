Mercedes-Benz

The inventor of the automobile, still setting the luxury standard.

Founded
1926
Founder
Karl Benz and Gottlieb Daimler (companies merged in 1926)
Headquarters
Stuttgart, Germany

History

Karl Benz patented the Benz Patent-Motorwagen in 1886, widely regarded as the first practical automobile. Benz & Cie. and Daimler-Motoren-Gesellschaft merged in 1926 to create Mercedes-Benz.

The S-Class has traditionally been the model where Mercedes-Benz introduces new safety and comfort technology, much of which later becomes standard across the industry.

Known for

  • The first practical automobile
  • The S-Class luxury flagship
  • AMG performance division
