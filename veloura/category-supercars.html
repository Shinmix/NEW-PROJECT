Supercars

Mid-engine machines built around extreme power, aerodynamics and lightweight engineering.

Supercars sit at the very top of road-car engineering. They use mid-mounted engines, carbon fibre structures and aerodynamics borrowed from motorsport to deliver acceleration and cornering ability that ordinary cars cannot approach.
