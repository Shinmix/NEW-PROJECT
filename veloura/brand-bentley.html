Bentley

British craftsmanship with grand touring performance.

Founded
1919
Founder
W. O. Bentley
Headquarters
Crewe, United Kingdom

History

W. O. Bentley founded the company in 1919, and the 'Bentley Boys' took the marque to five victories at the 24 Hours of Le Mans between 1924 and 1930. Bentley has been part of the Volkswagen Group since 1998.

The Crewe factory is still where Bentley interiors are trimmed, with wood veneers and leather hides prepared and fitted largely by hand.

Known for

  • Le Mans racing history
  • Hand-stitched interiors
  • The Continental GT grand tourer
