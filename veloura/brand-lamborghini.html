History

Ferruccio Lamborghini built his fortune manufacturing tractors before founding Automobili Lamborghini in 1963. His aim was to build a refined grand tourer that could rival the Ferraris of the day.

The Miura of 1966 established the mid-engine layout as the supercar template, and the Countach that followed created the sharp, angular design language the brand is still known for today.

Known for

  • Mid-engine V12 flagships
  • Scissor doors
  • Bold, angular styling
