Overview

The Ferrari 296 GTB is a two-seat, mid-rear-engine sports car introduced in 2021. It combines a 2,992 cc twin-turbocharged V6 with a plug-in hybrid electric motor for a combined output of 830 PS. The V6 is unusual in having a 120-degree angle between its cylinder banks, which allows the turbochargers to sit inside the V and keeps the engine low in the chassis.

Design

The 296 GTB is noticeably shorter than the V8 berlinettas that came before it, with a 2,600 mm wheelbase. Its rounded rear haunches and the recessed rear screen are a deliberate reference to the Ferrari 250 LM of the 1960s, while an active rear spoiler hidden in the bumper generates downforce only when it is needed.

Performance

Ferrari quotes 0-100 km/h in 2.9 seconds, 0-200 km/h in 7.3 seconds and a top speed of more than 330 km/h. The electric motor sits between the engine and the eight-speed dual-clutch gearbox, filling in torque while the turbochargers build boost.

Technology

A 7.45 kWh lithium-ion battery allows short journeys on electric power alone, which lets the car enter city zero-emission zones. Ferrari's eManettino selector lets the driver choose between electric, hybrid, performance and qualifying modes.

History

Unveiled on 24 June 2021, the 296 was the first series-production Ferrari with a six-cylinder engine since the Dino-branded 206 and 246 models, which were built by Ferrari but sold under the Dino name. A 296 GTS convertible and a track-focused 296 Speciale followed.

Interesting Facts

  • The name follows Ferrari tradition: 29 for the 2.9-litre displacement and 6 for the number of cylinders.
  • The 296 GT3 racing version won the 24 Hours of Nurburgring in 2023, its first year of competition.
  • It can be driven for roughly 25 km on electric power alone.
